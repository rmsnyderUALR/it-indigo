FAQ

The Root Cafe is a farm-to-table restaurant located in downtown Little Rock. This means that nearly all of the ingredients used for the menu are obtained from local farms and businesses.

This site was made to help determine what to cook for staff meal each night. I am the dinner lead so it falls to me to make sure my staff is fed and ready to work hard. I usually spend a lot of time deciding what to make because I care about making sure everyone's preferences and restrictions are accomodated as well as making a delicious meal. This site should shorten this decision making process drastically.
